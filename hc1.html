About me

I started cutting my friends' hair in the garage with a borrowed pair of clippers, and I never really stopped. Today I turn "just a haircut" into a little confidence boost — because when you look sharp, you walk differently.

My chair is a place for good conversation, clean lines and zero regrets. I love precision work: crisp edge-ups, smooth fades, and beards that actually suit your face.
